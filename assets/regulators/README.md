# Regulatory marks

The logo files in this folder are used in the “Across the regulatory landscape” strip in `../index.html`.

- `rbi.jpg` — Reserve Bank of India, downloaded from [rbi.org.in](https://rbi.org.in/images/rbi-company-logo.jpg).
- `sebi.png` — Securities and Exchange Board of India, downloaded from [sebi.gov.in](https://www.sebi.gov.in/images/sebi-logo.png).
- `irdai.svg` — Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India, downloaded from [irdai.gov.in](https://irdai.gov.in/o/irdai-theme/images/svg/logo/irdai-logo1.svg).
- `ifsca.png` — International Financial Services Centres Authority, downloaded from [ifsca.gov.in](https://ifsca.gov.in/images/logo.png).
- `mca.svg` — Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ministry_of_Corporate_Affairs_India.svg), by Swapnil1101, licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The asset is used without modification.

FEMA and GST are shown as labeled frameworks because they are not agencies with separate regulator logos.
